# Requests for retained analytical inputs

Requests should be sent to Xiaoyuan Bao at xybao@pku.edu.cn. Please provide your institution, the manifest paths of the requested files, the intended research or verification use, and any relevant source permissions already held. Do not send passwords, access tokens or information identifying participants.

The corresponding author will review the source and applicable terms for each requested file, including derived matrices and predictions. An existing licence may permit sharing; separate provider permission is needed only where the terms require it. If the terms are unclear, the author will seek clarification or direct the applicant to the original provider. Neither author approval nor an agreement with the recipient overrides third-party rights.

Only files that may lawfully be shared will be supplied. The author will record the decision, supporting source evidence, recipient and SHA-256 hashes of the delivered versions. Access to all 48 files and a fixed delivery period cannot be promised before that review. When a file cannot be supplied, the response will identify its source and the available acquisition instructions. Those instructions are not presented as a newly verified reconstruction of all historical inputs.
