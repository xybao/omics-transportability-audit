The Bioconductor pages displayed curatedTBData 2.8.0 and curatedMetagenomicData 3.20.0; they are package pages, not immutable links to the matrices used in this study. Record installed versions and session information when using these resources. A software licence does not necessarily cover every underlying dataset.

Accession-specific GEO records, including constituent accessions in pooled groups, are listed in SOURCE_ACCESS_CATALOG.md. Saved predictions and some processed inputs require additional steps beyond downloading provider files. EXACT_INPUTS.md and the reconstruction guide describe those distinctions. Follow the providers' terms and cite the source studies. The authors do not control the continued availability of these services.
