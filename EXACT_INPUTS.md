# Exact analytical inputs

Obtain third-party data from their original providers under the applicable terms. SOURCE_DOWNLOAD_LINKS.md distinguishes direct file links from resource pages, and SOURCE_ACCESS_CATALOG.md lists cohort accessions. INPUT_ACCESS_MANIFEST.csv and reproduction_code/ACCEPTED_INPUTS.json specify the 48 analytical inputs, including their paths and hashes.

The authors retain those inputs but do not publicly redistribute them. Requests can be sent to Xiaoyuan Bao (xybao@pku.edu.cn) and are assessed under source terms and any necessary provider permissions, as described in DATA_REQUEST_PROCEDURE.md. Availability cannot be guaranteed for every file.

Provider links do not directly supply every processed matrix or saved prediction. The package contains scientific code and aggregate results, but no private input archive or internal verification reports. INPUT_RECONSTRUCTION_STATUS.csv records the route for each file, and INPUT_RECONSTRUCTION_GUIDE.md explains the verified operations. Full reconstruction from provider downloads through historical model fitting has not been verified. The authors document the known acquisition routes without guaranteeing continued availability of external services.

Full scientific evaluation also needs the two auxiliary GEO metadata extracts in SOURCE_RECORDS.md. These are additional to the 48-input manifest and are not redistributed. Public figure and table replay uses 42 retained result CSVs and exports 24 numerical tables; S20 and S21 are excluded.
